Spicy noodle

Spicy noodle with chicken (or tofu), vegetables, rice noodles and a spicy sauce. Transcribed from a binder page; the source page lists ingredients only, with no written directions.

Noodles

  • About 3 nest of rice noodles

Spicy noodle

  • 2 chicken breasts (can use tofu)
  • 2 jalapeños (optional)
  • Various vegetables (about a cup or two)
  • 1 small oignon
  • Garlic and ginger
  • Peper

Sauce

  • 1/2 a cup chicken broth
  • 1/4 cup hoisin
  • 3 Tbs soy sauce
  • 2 Tbs sriracha
  • 2 tea spoon honey
  • 1 tea spoon sesame oil
